# Workflow: 04 Market Research

## Purpose
Systematic market and competitive intelligence investigation to validate product viability, pricing, and positioning using verified sources.

## Steps

### Step 1: Research Framing
- Define target audience, problem hypothesis, and target market segment.
- Formulate testable questions on competitor positioning and market dynamics.

### Step 2: Evidence Gathering
- Query authoritative sources, industry databases, and official documentation.
- Collect quantitative data points (market size, competitor pricing, feature benchmarks).

### Step 3: Synthesis & Market Sizing
- Calculate TAM, SAM, and SOM using explicit formulas and stated assumptions.
- Categorize claims as VERIFIED, ESTIMATED, HYPOTHESIS, or UNKNOWN.

### Step 4: Reporting
- Compile findings into `docs/templates/RESEARCH_REPORT.md`.
- Detail risks, barriers to entry, differentiation strategies, and next validation steps.
