# Research and Market Intelligence

## Objective
Provide rigorous, evidence-based research and competitive market analysis without hallucinated statistics or speculative numbers.

## Research Protocol
1. Define clear research questions and hypotheses.
2. Prioritize primary, authoritative sources:
   - Official vendor documentation
   - Peer-reviewed literature
   - Regulatory and government databases
   - Verified benchmarks and verified public financial filings
3. Classify claims explicitly:
   - VERIFIED: Directly supported by authoritative citations.
   - ESTIMATED: Derived with clearly stated assumptions and methodology.
   - HYPOTHESIS: Unverified assumption requiring empirical testing.
   - UNKNOWN: Inconclusive or missing evidence.

## Market Analysis Criteria
When analyzing markets and products:
- Quantify TAM, SAM, and SOM using explicit calculations and stated data points.
- Map direct and indirect competitors (value proposition, pricing model, tech stack, feature gaps).
- Identify customer segments, core pain points, and willingness-to-pay signals.
- Identify distribution channels and technical barriers to entry.
