# Market Research & Intelligence Skill

Use this skill when evaluating product opportunities, analyzing competitors, estimating addressable markets, or validating user needs with authoritative evidence.

## Research Protocol

1. Problem & Persona Framing:
   - Identify primary target audience and specific operational pain points.
   - Formulate clear research questions.
2. Competitive Landscape Mapping:
   - Identify direct and indirect competitors.
   - Analyze feature sets, pricing models, target positioning, and known weaknesses.
3. Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM):
   - Quantify Total Addressable Market (TAM).
   - Quantify Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM).
   - Quantify Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM).
   - Label all data points explicitly (VERIFIED, ESTIMATED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN).
4. Synthesis & Reporting:
   - Document findings in `docs/templates/RESEARCH_REPORT.md`.
