# MCR Transactional Email Deliverability & DNS Authentication Manual

---

## 1. DNS Authentication Records (Zero Spam Box Placement)
Never send production emails without configuring all 3 DNS authentication records:
1. **SPF (Sender Policy Framework)**:
   - Type: `TXT` on root (`@`)
   - Value: `v=spf1 include:resend.com ~all` (or SendGrid/SES equivalent)
2. **DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)**:
   - Type: `CNAME` or `TXT` with cryptographic public key generated by email provider.
   - Proves email body has not been intercepted or tampered with in transit.
3. **DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance)**:
   - Type: `TXT` on `_dmarc.yourdomain.com`
   - Value: `v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@yourdomain.com`

---

## 2. Rate Limiting OTP Generation (Anti-Abuse)
- Enforce strict IP and account rate limits on `/api/auth/send-otp`:
  - Maximum 1 OTP per 60 seconds per user.
  - Maximum 5 OTP attempts per hour per IP.
- Block disposable/temporary email provider domains (`tempmail.com`, `mailinator.com`).
