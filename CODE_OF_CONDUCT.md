# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in **MCROS**, created by **Maheshwar Chandra Roy (MCR)**, a harassment-free and professional engineering experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy, professional kindness, and respect towards other engineers.
- Focused, technical discussions driven by evidence and objective benchmark results.
- Gracefully accepting constructive technical criticism.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Frivolous chitchat, romantic flirting, inappropriate sexual language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting technical contributions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community maintainer at support@mcros.dev.
